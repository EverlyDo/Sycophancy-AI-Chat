You are being invited to volunteer to participate in a research study.
This summary explains information about this research.

Study Aims: You are invited to take part in a research study that examines how people perceive and evaluate relationship advisors. If you agree to participate, you will be asked to read a brief scenario, interact through a chat interface, and then answer survey questions about your impressions of the interaction.
These activities are similar to everyday situations where people get opinions or receive advice. Your participation will take approximately 15-20 minutes. This study involves procedures that you will not be fully informed about in advance. You will be provided with a complete explanation of all study procedures and their purpose at the end of your participation.

Data Collection: Your activity during the study will be recorded for research purposes. To protect your privacy, no personally identifiable information will be collected. All recorded data will be de-identified prior to analysis and stored securely for the duration of the study. Only authorized members of the research team will have access to the data. All information you provide will be kept strictly confidential. This study involves no more than minimal risk.

Your participation in this survey is voluntary. You can choose not to take part in this study. If you decide to participate, you can change your mind later and leave the study at any time. You can skip any questions that you don't want to answer. You will not be penalized or lose any benefits if you decide not to participate or choose to leave the study later.

If you have questions, complaints, or concerns about the research, please do not hesitate to contact the PI. If you have questions regarding your rights as a research subject or concerns regarding your privacy, you may contact the Human Research Protection Program at 814-865-1775.

By continuing with this questionnaire, you are acknowledging that you have read the information summarizing the study, and you are consenting to participate in this research.
